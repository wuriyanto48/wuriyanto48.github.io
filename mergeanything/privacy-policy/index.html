Privacy Policy for Merge Anything

Effective Date: September 3, 2026

Merge Anything (“we,” “our,” or “us”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information about you is collected, used, and disclosed when you play our mobile application Merge Anything (the “Game”).

By downloading, installing, or playing the Game, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Do Not Collect Directly

We respect your personal privacy. We do not require you to create an account, register, or provide any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address to play the Game.

All game progression and save data are stored locally on your device.

2. Information Collected Automatically by Third-Party Services

While we do not collect personal data directly, the Game integrates third-party Software Development Kits (SDKs) to serve advertisements. These third parties may collect non-personal or pseudonymous data automatically to function properly.

Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob to display rewarded video advertisements (e.g., to unlock extra game features like additional shakes). Google AdMob may collect and process information such as:

  • Unique Device Identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID / AAID).
  • IP Address and general location data.
  • Device Information (device model, operating system version, system language).
  • Ad Interaction Data (impressions, clicks, and diagnostic data).

This data is used solely to deliver personalized or non-personalized advertisements, prevent fraud, and analyze ad performance.

You can read more about how Google handles your data in the Google Privacy & Terms.

3. How We Use Information

Any information processed through third-party services is used exclusively to:

  • Provide and maintain core game functionalities.
  • Deliver rewarded advertisements requested by the user.
  • Ensure game stability, security, and fraud prevention.

4. Children’s Privacy

The Game is designed for general audiences and does not knowingly target or collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or 16 in certain jurisdictions). If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can take necessary actions.

5. Data Security

Since your game progress is stored locally on your mobile device, the security of your game data depends on your device’s security settings. Third-party ad networks (Google AdMob) employ industry-standard encryption and security measures to protect data during transmission.

6. Your Choices & Opt-Outs

You can manage or reset your Advertising ID settings at any time through your mobile device settings:

  • Android: Settings $\rightarrow$ Privacy $\rightarrow$ Ads $\rightarrow$ Reset or Delete Advertising ID.
  • iOS: Settings $\rightarrow$ Privacy & Security $\rightarrow$ Tracking.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Effective Date.” You are advised to review this page periodically for any updates.
