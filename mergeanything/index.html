Merge Anything

Merge, Shake, and Discover Whimsical Physics Combinations!

Merge Anything is a casual 2D physics puzzle game where you drop and merge unique objects, leverage natural gravity, and use the tactile Shake feature to jumble up the board whenever you get stuck!

🎮 Key Features

  • Physics-Based Merging: Experience satisfying object combination driven by responsive 2D physics and gravity mechanics.
  • Tactile Shake Ability: Board feeling clogged up? Use the Shake ability to toss objects around and create brand-new space!
  • Diverse Visual Themes: Unlock and enjoy multiple vibrant themes, ranging from sweet bakeries to unique daily items.
  • Free to Play: Experience the full gameplay loop for free, with optional Rewarded Ads available to grab extra abilities when you need them.
